关键数据点:
  • leader_changes: 应该=0 (正常没有变更)
  • election_triggered: 应该=1 (初始选举)
  • heartbeat_timeouts: 应该=0 (无超时)
  • entries_per_second: 应该>0 (有写入时)
  • rtt_avg_ms: 同区<10ms, 跨区150-250ms
  • packet_loss_percent: 应该≈0%
  • tcp_retransmits_total: 应该很低
